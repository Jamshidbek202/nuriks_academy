# Privacy Policy

**Nurik's Academy**

**Last Updated:** June 2025

## 1. Introduction

Nurik's Academy ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information
We collect information you provide directly:
- Name and contact details (email, phone number)
- Account credentials (username, password)
- Student and parent information
- Payment information
- Profile photos

### 2.2 Usage Information
We automatically collect:
- Device information (model, OS version)
- App usage statistics
- Attendance records
- Academic performance data
- Chat messages and communications

### 2.3 Push Notification Data
With your consent, we collect:
- Device push notification tokens
- Notification preferences
- Notification interaction data

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
- Provide educational management services
- Process payments and billing
- Send notifications about homework, tests, and lessons
- Communicate between students, teachers, and parents
- Improve our services and user experience
- Ensure platform security

## 4. Information Sharing

We may share information with:
- Teachers and school staff (academic information)
- Parents/guardians (student information)
- Payment processors (billing information)
- Service providers (hosting, analytics)

We DO NOT sell your personal information to third parties.

## 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
- Secure password storage (bcrypt hashing)
- JWT-based authentication
- Role-based access control
- Regular security audits

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your data for:
- Active accounts: Duration of enrollment
- Academic records: 7 years after graduation
- Payment records: As required by law
- Chat history: 2 years

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request data deletion
- Opt-out of marketing communications
- Disable push notifications
- Export your data

## 8. Children's Privacy

Our service is designed for educational institutions. For users under 13, we require parental consent and provide parents access to their child's information.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred and stored on servers located outside your country. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email.

## 11. Contact Us

For privacy-related inquiries:
- **Email:** privacy@nuriksacademy.com
- **App:** Settings > Support
